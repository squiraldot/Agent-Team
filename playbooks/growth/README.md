# Growth Playbook

## Goal
Run ethical, measurable distribution experiments without misleading users or spamming them.

## Workflow
1. Define audience, value proposition, channel, and hypothesis.
2. Choose one primary metric and guardrail metrics.
3. Record baseline, experiment duration, sample assumptions, and stop condition.
4. Produce copy/assets with truthful claims and clear disclosures.
5. Reviewer checks brand fit, accessibility, consent, platform rules, and claims.
6. Human owner approves launch.
7. Analyze outcomes and document what did/did not work.

## Guardrails
- No fake testimonials, fabricated urgency, purchased engagement, or undisclosed sponsorship.
- No unsolicited bulk outreach or scraping personal contact details.
- Respect opt-outs, rate limits, privacy, and platform policies.
- Do not call an experiment successful without sufficient observed data.

## Output
Hypothesis; audience; variants; metric plan; risks; approval state; results; next decision.
