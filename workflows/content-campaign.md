# Content campaign pipeline

**Use for:** a coordinated series of posts, articles, scripts, email content or launch assets.

**Standards:** [content and editorial](../docs/standards/content.md), [graphic branding](../docs/standards/graphic-branding.md), [illustration](../docs/standards/illustration.md) when visuals are in scope, and [deliverable formats](../docs/DELIVERABLE_FORMATS.md).

## Ordered roles and handoffs

1. **Orchestrator** — [agents/orchestrator/role.md](../agents/orchestrator/role.md): objective, audience, channels, schedule, voice, CTA, budget and approval owner.
2. **Planner** — [agents/planner/role.md](../agents/planner/role.md): campaign brief, content matrix, milestones, success metrics and review gates.
3. **Deep Researcher + Content Creator** — [Deep Researcher](../agents/deep-researcher/role.md) and [Content Creator](../agents/content-creator/role.md): verify factual claims, current trends and source/licensing constraints; pass evidence log and caveats.
4. **Content Creator** — [role contract](../agents/content-creator/role.md): channel-specific drafts, scripts, titles, captions, CTAs, accessibility text and source attribution.
5. **Graphic Designer & Branding / Illustrator & Visual Artist** — [branding](../agents/graphic-designer-branding/role.md) and [illustration](../agents/illustrator-visual-artist/role.md) roles, when visuals are in scope: visual brief and channel-ready assets.
6. **QA/Reviewer** — [role contract](../agents/qa-reviewer/role.md): fact, tone, brand, platform-format, accessibility, attribution and consistency review.
7. **Content Creator / Builder** — revise, check links and exports, prepare publishing package and tracking plan.
8. **Orchestrator** — handoff final assets, channel specs, source notes, schedule and outstanding approvals.

## Quality gates

- Claims are supported or clearly framed as opinion/uncertainty; no fabricated quotes, sources, metrics or testimonials.
- Confirm rights to assets/music/quotes and platform limits where material.
- Verify spelling, links, captions/subtitles, image text and channel dimensions.
- SEO metadata describes the actual page; ranking and snippet-display guarantees are prohibited.
- Publishing, sending, paid promotion, or impersonation-sensitive content requires human approval.
- Mark each artifact draft, fact-checked, approved, or published only when that status is true.

## Parallelizable work

Draft channel variants from an approved message map in parallel; research claims and build the publishing checklist concurrently. Use one source-of-truth message map to prevent conflicting promises.
