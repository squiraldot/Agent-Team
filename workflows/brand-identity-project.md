# Brand identity project pipeline

**Use for:** naming, positioning, identity systems, logos, visual language and brand guidelines.

**Standards:** [graphic design and branding](../docs/standards/graphic-branding.md), [illustration](../docs/standards/illustration.md) for custom visuals, and [deliverable formats](../docs/DELIVERABLE_FORMATS.md). Use [brand-kit layout](../templates/brand-kit/README.md).

## Ordered roles and handoffs

1. **Orchestrator** — [agents/orchestrator/role.md](../agents/orchestrator/role.md): business purpose, audience, usage contexts, constraints, stakeholders and approval owner.
2. **Planner** — [agents/planner/role.md](../agents/planner/role.md): deliverable list, success criteria, rollout surfaces, research needs and review milestones.
3. **Deep Researcher** — [agents/deep-researcher/role.md](../agents/deep-researcher/role.md): category conventions, audience context, competitor differentiation and preliminary name/domain/trademark screening boundaries; pass evidence and caveats.
4. **Graphic Designer & Branding** — [agents/graphic-designer-branding/role.md](../agents/graphic-designer-branding/role.md): strategy, concepts, logo variants, typography/color system, responsive usage and brand guide.
5. **Illustrator & Visual Artist** — [agents/illustrator-visual-artist/role.md](../agents/illustrator-visual-artist/role.md), if custom illustration or image assets are needed: art direction, asset briefs and consistency checks.
6. **QA/Reviewer** — [agents/qa-reviewer/role.md](../agents/qa-reviewer/role.md): independent critique for legibility, distinctiveness, accessibility, scalability, cultural/contextual risk and deliverable completeness.
7. **Graphic Designer & Branding** — refine concepts and export/organize final assets; document file formats, variants, clear space and misuse guidance.
8. **Orchestrator** — handoff source/editable files where available, exports, specifications, usage guide, open legal checks and approval status.

## Quality gates

- Test legibility at small sizes, monochrome, light/dark backgrounds and intended applications.
- Check contrast, font/stock licensing, editable-source availability and provenance.
- Similarity research is not legal clearance; escalate trademark or naming risk to a qualified professional.
- Final adoption, public rollout or paid production requires stakeholder approval.
- Use a manifest that distinguishes source masters, exports, mockups, and files not yet supplied.

## Parallelizable work

Research can map competitor conventions while the designer explores concept directions once positioning is fixed. Separate illustration assets can be developed concurrently with clear style/token ownership.
