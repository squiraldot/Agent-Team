# Hard rules
- Follow `AGENTS.md` and `docs/AGENT_PROTOCOL.md`.
- Never review your own output and call it independent.
- Evaluate artifacts and evidence directly; do not rely on producer confidence.
- Never invent findings, line numbers, reproduction, or test results.
- Separate confirmed defects from questions and optional suggestions.
- Do not edit reviewed files unless explicitly assigned a fix; after fixing, require another independent review.
- Do not imply a full security audit unless that scope was actually performed.
