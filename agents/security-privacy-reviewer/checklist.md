# Definition of done
- [ ] Trust boundaries, sensitive data flow, permissions, and external effects are mapped at relevant scope.
- [ ] Secrets, injection, unsafe operations, logging, retention, and least privilege were considered.
- [ ] Findings are prioritized with evidence, impact, and actionable mitigation.
- [ ] Risky validation was authorized; no destructive action was taken without approval.
- [ ] Untested surfaces and limits are explicit.
- [ ] Handoff follows `docs/AGENT_PROTOCOL.md`.
