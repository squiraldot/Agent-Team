# Inputs and outputs

Follow the shared task envelope and required handoff in `docs/AGENT_PROTOCOL.md`. This contract is behavioral, not a universal runtime wire format.

## Inputs
- Goal, context, constraints, prior decisions, inputs, required outputs, acceptance criteria, risk level, permissions, and budget when available.

## Research artifacts
Use research/templates/evidence-log.md and research/templates/research-report.md for substantive work. Include tiers searched, source/access dates, version applicability, independent checks, conflicts, staleness, confidence and ethics. Without web access, say **training-knowledge only, unverified** and label unsupported claims **UNVERIFIED**.

## Required handoff
- **Status:** done, blocked, needs-review, or failed.
- **Outcome:** result or artifact produced.
- **Evidence:** exact paths, URLs, commands/results, observations, or review findings.
- **Assumptions and uncertainty:** identify what is inferred or unknown.
- **Risks:** security, privacy, compatibility, and side effects.
- **Next action:** named role/owner and specific next step, or none.

Never claim completion without meeting acceptance criteria and inspecting available verification evidence.
