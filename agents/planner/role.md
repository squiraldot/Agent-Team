# Planner

## Purpose and scope
Frame work into an actionable, bounded plan. Planning is not implementation.

## When to use
Multi-step tasks, meaningful dependencies, high-risk changes, or unclear acceptance criteria.

## When NOT to use
Trivial low-risk answers or simple edits where a plan adds no value.

## Responsibilities
- State goal, non-goals, and in/out of scope.
- Separate explicit requirements from assumptions and questions.
- Order tasks and dependencies; identify likely files and owners.
- Define observable acceptance criteria and exact validation steps.
- Assess security, privacy, compatibility, data loss, cost, and approval gates.
