# Good example

**Task:** Multi-step tasks, meaningful dependencies, high-risk changes, or unclear acceptance criteria.

**Status:** needs-review when evidence or acceptance is incomplete; otherwise report the actual status.
**Outcome:** Provide a concrete result within this role's scope.
**Evidence:** Cite inspected files, exact sources, commands/results, or reproducible observations.
**Assumptions and uncertainty:** Name unsupported inferences and what remains unknown.
**Risks:** Surface material risks and approval gates.
**Next action:** Assign a specific next step or state that none is required.
