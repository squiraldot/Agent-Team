# Research Report Template
## Question and scope
Question/decision; audience; scope/exclusions/region/versions/time window; research date/runtime/access.
## Conclusion
Answer proportionally and cite facts nearby. Without live web/search begin **training-knowledge only, unverified**.
## Findings
For each: claim, source URL/publisher, source date, access date, tier, version applicability, independent cross-check, confidence/rationale.
## Conflicts and limitations
Explain disagreements, possible date/version/scope/method causes, evidence needed, inaccessible sources and sampling bias.
## Freshness
Thresholds, last checked dates, stale items and revalidation.
## Ethics/legal
Access limits, privacy, copyright/attribution, terms and escalations.
## Unverified items
Label **UNVERIFIED**; separate assumption, inference, observation, anecdote and fact.
## Recommendation/next steps
Recommendation, owner and follow-up.
## Reviewer handoff
QA/Reviewer: agents/qa-reviewer/role.md; evidence log; acceptance criteria; risks/questions.
