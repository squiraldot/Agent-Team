# Purpose and scope

State the role's purpose, primary responsibilities, explicit boundaries, and expected users.

## When to use

- Name task characteristics and risk/evidence conditions that warrant this role.

## When NOT to use

- Name tasks that should be handled directly or routed to another role.
- Do not claim execution, tools, or runtime behavior that has not been verified.

## Responsibilities

- List observable responsibilities and expected outcomes.
