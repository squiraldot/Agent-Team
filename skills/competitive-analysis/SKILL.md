# Competitive Analysis

## Trigger conditions
Trigger when evaluating market positioning, product gaps, pricing, feature prioritization, or go-to-market options.

## Steps
1. State the decision, market/geography, target segment, time window, and competitor selection rationale.
2. Use official product/pricing/docs plus credible independent sources; respect access terms and privacy.
3. Capture dated evidence for positioning, audience, features, pricing, distribution, strengths, limitations, and differentiators.
4. Separate directly observed facts, vendor claims, customer anecdotes, and analyst inference.
5. Compare against explicit criteria; include substitutes and the option of doing nothing.
6. Identify gaps, risks, counter-evidence, confidence, and likely response from competitors.
7. Recommend next experiments and refresh triggers; do not infer private strategy from speculation.

## Checklist
- [ ] Decision and comparison criteria explicit
- [ ] Competitor selection justified
- [ ] Claims dated and sourced
- [ ] Facts separated from interpretation
- [ ] Substitutes and counter-evidence included
- [ ] Recommendation linked to evidence and uncertainty

## Failure modes
Stale or incomparable pricing/features: mark unknown and recheck. Small sample of reviews: do not generalize. Unsupported market-size claims or speculation: omit or clearly qualify.
