# Content Calendar

## Trigger conditions
Trigger when planning a campaign, monthly/quarterly publishing, channel mix, or consistent editorial operations.

## Steps
1. Define audience, business goal, funnel stage, channels, cadence, owner, budget, and review constraints.
2. Review audience questions, search/community evidence, seasonality, prior performance, and competitor coverage without copying.
3. Build content pillars and map each idea to intent, format, channel, CTA, and measurable hypothesis.
4. Schedule realistic production, fact-check, design, approval, localization, and repurposing steps.
5. Record source evidence, dependencies, publication date/time zone, responsible owner, and status.
6. Add accessibility, rights, disclosure, privacy, and platform-policy checks.
7. Review results at a defined cadence and adjust using evidence rather than vanity metrics alone.

## Checklist
- [ ] Goals and audience defined
- [ ] Cadence fits available capacity
- [ ] Each item has owner, format, CTA, and KPI
- [ ] Evidence and rights checks included
- [ ] Approval and publication dates have time zone
- [ ] Review cadence and decision rules defined

## Failure modes
Unverified trend or date: label hypothesis and verify before publishing. Capacity mismatch: reduce cadence, not review quality. Engagement without goal impact: revisit KPI and distribution.
