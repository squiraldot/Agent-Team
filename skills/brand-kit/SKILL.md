# Brand Kit

## Trigger conditions
Trigger when launching/repositioning a brand or preparing reusable identity assets for web, apps, social, or print.

## Steps
1. Capture audience, positioning, competitors, values, differentiation, usage contexts, and constraints.
2. Research naming/trademark/domain conflicts where relevant; state limits and avoid legal clearance claims.
3. Define brand attributes, voice principles, and message hierarchy.
4. Specify primary/secondary/submark/icon logo variants, clear space, minimum sizes, backgrounds, and misuse examples.
5. Define color values with contrast pairings, typography hierarchy, imagery/icon style, and layout principles.
6. Export correctly sized formats and document source/editable files, licensing, and usage.
7. Test assets at favicon/mobile/print/social sizes and against accessibility needs.

## Checklist
- [ ] Audience and positioning documented
- [ ] Logo variants and usage rules defined
- [ ] Color values and contrast pairings recorded
- [ ] Typography and voice guidance included
- [ ] Export formats and source files accounted for
- [ ] Licensing and trademark limitations stated

## Failure modes
Name availability not checked: label as unverified. Logo illegible at small sizes: simplify and retest. Missing source/license details: do not hand off as production-ready.
