# Illustration & Visual Art Standards

## Principles
Start with message, audience, placement and art direction. Track planned/generated/edited/approved status honestly. Respect consent, copyright, culture and likeness rights; do not request exact imitation of a living artist.

## Process
Brief subject/story/placement/ratio/dimensions; gather permitted references and record rights; specify focal point, composition, palette, lighting, mood and exclusions; use ../../templates/image-brief.md; record tool/model/version only when known; inspect artifacts, text, crop and continuity; deliver outputs/source only if available with provenance and limitations; independent review.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Subject reads at intended size; dimensions, ratio, crop and transparency match.
- [ ] Series palette and continuity consistent.
- [ ] No unwanted text/watermarks or obvious artifacts.
- [ ] References authorized; alt text/decorative status specified.
- [ ] Formats, filenames, licenses and limitations listed; unknowns UNVERIFIED.

## Common pitfalls
Ambiguous prompts, exact-text expectations, forgotten crop/rights, prompt passed off as image, raster passed off as editable vector, assuming output is inherently accurate or safe.
