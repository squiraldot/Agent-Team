# Graphic Design and Branding Standards

**Status:** Active guidance. Revalidate by 2027-01-28.  
**Research protocol:** Follow [research/PROTOCOL.md](../../research/PROTOCOL.md). Sources accessed 2026-10-28. Trademark clearance, audience response, and asset licence scope remain **UNVERIFIED** until checked for the specific project.

## Principles
- Tie visual choices to positioning, audience, use cases, and differentiators; avoid decoration without rationale.
- Build a system: primary/secondary marks, submark, favicon/app icon, typography, semantic color, imagery, layout, and usage rules.
- Design for legibility, recognition, contrast, reproduction, and cropping across real sizes, backgrounds, print, and screens.
- Separate editable masters, exports, previews, and mockups. Promise source/editability only when the source exists.
- Record provenance, licences, font terms, and third-party assets. Similarity research is not legal clearance.
- Treat generated output as a candidate until reviewed for artifacts, rights, brand fit, and production needs.

## Process
1. **Discover:** gather goals, audience, category, competitors, positioning, tone, placements, production methods, constraints, and approver.
2. **Research:** consult official identity guidance and credible category/context references. Log URLs, access dates, asset origin, licence, and caveats.
3. **Define:** write strategy and design criteria; establish naming, voice, color roles, typography, and desired differentiation.
4. **Explore:** create meaningfully distinct concepts and explain rationale/trade-offs, not just cosmetic variations.
5. **Systematize:** specify logo variants, clear space, minimum size, monochrome/reversed use, tokens, typography, imagery, grid, and misuse examples.
6. **Test:** inspect small-size, monochrome, light/dark, contrast, crop, screen, and print use as applicable.
7. **Package:** follow [brand-kit layout](../../templates/brand-kit/README.md); include available vector masters, exports, tokens, font/licence notes, usage guide, and manifest.
8. **Independent review:** check consistency, contrast, file integrity, provenance, licensing, and deliverable claims.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Brief states audience, positioning, contexts, constraints, success criteria, and approver.
- [ ] Logo variants and intended uses are documented; clear space/minimum-size guidance is tested.
- [ ] Palette includes exact digital values; print values are supplied only when validated. Contrast is checked for relevant uses.
- [ ] Font names, weights, fallbacks, availability, and licence terms are recorded.
- [ ] SVGs are scalable, sensibly named, and free from unintended raster/external dependencies.
- [ ] Exports cover agreed transparent/opaque, light/dark, and aspect-ratio variants.
- [ ] Assets have provenance/licence notes; unknown rights are marked UNVERIFIED.
- [ ] Guide includes real placements and misuse examples.
- [ ] Legal/trademark review is distinguished from design QA and stakeholder approval.

## Common pitfalls
Choosing a logo before positioning; copying competitors; claiming name/domain/trademark availability from a quick search; treating a raster mockup as an editable vector master; untested contrast; assuming RGB values are validated CMYK/Pantone specs; missing font/stock licences; exporting one oversized SVG with unnecessary filters or external resources; confusing preference with evidence of market recognition.
